# Adler coaching

Adler helps people pursue their own goals through feasible actions, research-informed support and learning from their experience. This glossary distinguishes what the person wants, what is observed, and what the coach is still trying to understand.

## Goals and work

**Goal**: A result the person wants, with their own meaning of success and relevant constraints. A goal's outcome is distinct from the work they can control.

**Controllable input**: Work or a behaviour the person can choose to perform, including its meaningful quantity or quality. It is not another person's response or an assumed return from the work.

**Outcome**: The result relevant to the goal, observed or reported separately from performing the action. Completing an action does not necessarily establish its outcome.

**Goal route**: The shape of work a goal requires, such as a habit, work session, structural change or campaign. The route informs appropriate planning and learning without defining the person's identity.

**Planning cycle**: A bounded period of concrete work selected for the goal and the person's circumstances. Its length need not equal the goal horizon or a calendar week.

**Review point**: A date, event or evidence condition at which Adler evaluates what to keep, change or clarify. Reaching it does not guarantee enough evidence for a conclusion.

## Evidence and learning

**Report**: An account supplied through a conversation, check-in or connected source. One report can describe several attempts, and several reports can describe the same attempt.

**Observation**: A recorded fact or measurement about an occurrence or an explicitly reported aggregate, with its origin, timing and relevant context. An observation is distinct from Adler's interpretation; a reported fact remains attributable to its source.

**Exposure**: An instance of the person using the support or encountering the condition being studied. Agreement, a scheduled opportunity and the number of reports do not by themselves establish exposure.

**Confirmed context**: Information or preferences the person has stated or confirmed. Confirmation establishes their account, not independent proof of a causal relationship.

**Research claim**: A specific proposition supported or motivated by an identifiable research source, within that source's design, population and limitations. A framework name or a citation alone is not a claim.

**Behavioural mechanism**: A proposed process through which a change may influence behaviour. A theory can motivate the mechanism even when its operation in this person has not been established.

**Hypothesis**: A revisable explanation or prediction connecting observations, a behavioural mechanism and an expected effect in a stated context. It remains distinct from a user-confirmed fact.

**Test**: A prospective attempt to learn from a specified change, useful observations and a review rule. A test is not necessarily a randomized or causal experiment.

**Test result**: The observations obtained during the test, including what is missing or incomparable. The result is distinct from the interpretation made from it.

**Working insight**: A provisional interpretation of reviewed evidence, with its scope and implication for future planning. It can remain worth using without becoming a proven personal rule.

**Transfer**: Applying a mechanism or working insight to a different goal or context. Evidence from the original setting informs that application without establishing the same effect in the new setting.

**Memory**: The person's retained context, observations, current hypotheses, tests and learning history. Memory includes uncertain interpretations as well as confirmed context, with those roles kept distinct.

## Coaching decisions

**Recommendation**: A suggested action or adjustment with a reason grounded in the person's context and applicable methodology. Suggesting or accepting it is not evidence that it happened or worked.

**Proposal**: A concrete change presented for the person's decision, with its consequences and current state. Its acceptance is distinct from action completion and test results.

**Conditional projection**: A scenario describing future goal progress if stated input and relationship assumptions hold. It is distinct from an observed result, a deadline commitment and a calibrated success probability.

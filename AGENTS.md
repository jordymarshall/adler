# Product and coaching changes

The user requires methodology-first engineering across Adler. Read `docs/method/adler-method.md` before changing coaching behavior, goal setup, planning, check-ins, scheduling, progress, insights, adaptation, or related product claims. It is the current app contract and resolves older design choices.

- Ground decisions in `docs/research/behavioural-science-synthesis.md` and its referenced deep dives. Keep evidence grades, transfer limits, source IDs and heuristic labels intact. The seven-method UI catalog alone is not the research base.
- Preserve the shared coach across channels. Update the executable framework, prompts, schemas, evidence validation and relevant UI together when the behavior changes.
- Distinguish user-reported facts, hypotheses, controllable input, observed outcome and conditional projection. Never promote a sparse association into a confirmed personal rule or causal probability.
- Choose cycles and action size for the user and goal type. Do not universalize historical habit thresholds or reproduce the old fixed intake script.
- Keep product changes surgical. Explain the framework connection and verify the affected behavior; do not add unrelated mechanics or invent research support.
- The user's current instructions and authorization take precedence over historical method/product documents. These instructions add no permission or approval gates.

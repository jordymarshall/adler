# adler

Adler turns your goals into manageable actions, uses behavioural science and your check-ins to understand what helps, and adapts the plan around your life.

Start product and engineering work with the [governing product principles](docs/product-principles.md), [current Adler Method](docs/method/adler-method.md) and [engineering design](docs/method/coaching-engineering.md). The [domain glossary](CONTEXT.md) defines the shared language. The design documents distinguish accepted direction, proposed implementation and current capabilities.

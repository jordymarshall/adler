# Adler's governing product direction

Accepted user direction · 7 September 2026

This is standing guidance for future product and engineering iterations. It records the user's accepted direction; it is not a claim that every capability below is implemented. The [current method](method/adler-method.md) describes the runtime contract and known limits. The [engineering design](method/coaching-engineering.md) specifies how to build toward this direction.

## Product core

Adler turns the person's goals into manageable actions, learns what helps or gets in the way, and adapts the system around their life. The value depends on the quality of its behavioural science and its ability to apply that science to this person's circumstances.

The user should experience useful guidance, understand the reason that matters and be able to inspect its depth. They should not have to administer the methodology, choose scientific frameworks or operate an experiment dashboard to get help.

## One shared coaching system

The product is one engineered system: research, prompts, model policy, deterministic rules, tools, state, conversations and memory. Web chat, app controls, SMS/iMessage, MCP and background reviews use that same system whenever interpretation or planning is required. They present it differently while sharing authoritative records, capabilities and authorization.

Simple commands can be deterministic. They still use shared validation and maintain the same evidence and learning state. Channel-specific prompts, duplicated memory or alternate reasoning paths must not create different coaches.

## Specific behavioural science is required

Every substantive Adler recommendation and behavioural interpretation must be traceable to specific claims in the owned framework and research, with the person's evidence and the application explained. A framework name, generic scientific language or a plausible citation is insufficient.

The method must distinguish the theoretical mechanism, empirical evidence for a technique, the person's observations and Adler's tentative application. Retain claim/source IDs, source excerpts and versions, evidence grades, population and transfer limits, competing explanations and heuristics. The displayed sentence is a short expression of the supported decision record; it is not a substitute for that record.

Research informs the coach's inference and selection before a recommendation is made. It must not be attached afterward merely to make an already chosen answer appear scientific. A semantic review must check whether the cited claims actually support the relevant premises and whether their application is defensible.

## Live learning is part of memory

Current hypotheses and tests are durable, goal-linked records. They preserve the observation, behavioural interpretation, expected effect, change tried, test state, review timing, feedback and resulting understanding. Each revision retains its earlier basis.

The coach reads and updates this memory across interfaces. Learning can inform other goals, with the original scope and transfer uncertainty preserved. User-confirmed context, a working hypothesis and an observed outcome remain distinct.

Review timing, test design and action size depend on the goal and meaningful feedback. Acceptance does not mean an action happened; a review date does not prove a hypothesis; absent feedback does not justify a conclusion. Corrected or outdated evidence changes the authority of dependent learning.

## Simple guidance with access to depth

A meaningful recommendation leads with the action and clear accept/decline/discuss/edit controls where a pending recommendation needs a decision. Already-authorized or applied changes show their actual state and correction controls. Underneath, use short, distinguishable statements of observation, behavioural science and expected effect. The behavioural statement links directly to its specific methodological basis. Detailed sources, applicability, alternatives and history are easy to inspect.

Keep consequential uncertainty and the effect of accepting a change visible. Routine check-ins stay brief. Today makes the next useful action apparent; goal detail makes the current plan and trajectory understandable; Insights shows attributable observations and preferences, current tests, review timelines and what learning changes in the plan. Useful context and insights can exist without a test; their evidence and status must remain clear.

## Measure useful work and results honestly

Controllable inputs, action adherence, reported outcomes and conditional projections answer different questions. Preserve their meaning in every interface. Missing evidence remains unknown. A change in measurement cannot silently inherit the old relationship.

Keep the outcome-versus-time graph, target and conditional forward range where the relationship supports them. Complex outcomes can have an unknown relationship and no defensible finish date. A shaded range is not automatically a calibrated interval, and behavioural literature cannot establish a personal dollars-per-hour return.

## Low friction through product understanding

The landing page demonstrates a specific check-in, useful adjustment and later learning early, then shows how the features support that experience. Use readable parts of the real product, clear visual focus and controllable motion. The value should remain understandable without enlarging a screenshot or waiting for animation.

Onboarding reaches a feasible first action using only information needed for the next decision. Later use develops the personal context. Connections and advanced configuration appear when useful. The person should understand what they contribute and what Adler manages.

## Quality is in the implementation

For each iteration, design the best defensible way to deliver the intended user outcome, then select the simplest implementation that meets that standard. Be willing to build capabilities the product requires; do not replace necessary scientific reasoning with a familiar UI pattern or extra prompt wording.

Specify the evidence, decision process, data semantics, failure cases, user control and observable acceptance criteria before calling the work complete. Keep usability, methodological fidelity, model reliability and actual coaching effectiveness as separate evaluation questions. Longer reasoning, more citations or more sophisticated statistics are not evidence of better coaching by themselves.

The [accepted experience plan](product-core-and-experience-plan.md) and [original review](scientific-disclosure-product-proposal.md) supply concrete examples and identified gaps. Current user instructions take precedence. This guidance creates no additional approval gates.
